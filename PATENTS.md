# OpenAPPL Patent Commitment

> **Status.** The owner approved this commitment on 2026-11-13. It states the commitment
> in plain language and takes effect once counsel has reviewed it; this notice is then
> removed.

## 1. The commitment

Mindbricks Labs ("the Steward") wants anyone to be able to implement OpenAPPL without
fear of patent claims from the Steward. Therefore:

**The Steward irrevocably promises not to assert any Necessary Claim against any person
or organisation for making, using, selling, offering for sale, importing or distributing
a Conforming Implementation, to the extent the implementation conforms to a Covered
Version of the Specification.**

This promise is given free of charge, without the need to sign anything or to notify the
Steward, and to everyone: open-source and commercial implementers, users of
implementations, and their customers.

## 2. Definitions

- **Specification**: the OpenAPPL ontology, language specification and compiler
  specification, and the schema `openappl.json`, as published by the Steward.
- **Covered Version**: every version of the Specification published by the Steward while
  this commitment is in effect. A version, once covered, stays covered.
- **Conforming Implementation**: software, or a part of it, that reads, writes, validates,
  transforms or compiles OpenAPPL blueprints, or that is generated from a blueprint by a
  compiler, to the extent it implements the required or optional behaviour of a Covered
  Version.
- **Necessary Claims**: the claims of patents and patent applications, anywhere in the
  world, owned or controlled by the Steward now or in the future, that would necessarily
  be infringed by implementing a Covered Version, because no technically reasonable,
  non-infringing alternative exists. It does not include claims to technology that the
  Specification only refers to or relies on (for example, a database engine, a message
  broker or a payment provider), or to parts of an implementation that the Specification
  does not describe.

## 3. Defensive termination

The promise does not apply to anyone who, while the promise would otherwise protect them,
files or joins a patent infringement claim against the Steward, or against any other
implementer, alleging that a Conforming Implementation infringes a patent. For that party
the promise ends from the date the claim is filed, for all versions.

## 4. Successors and transfers

The promise binds the Steward's successors and anyone to whom the Steward transfers a
Necessary Claim. The Steward will make any such transfer subject to this commitment.

## 5. Contributors

Contributors who add material to the Specification make the same promise for their own
Necessary Claims that read on the material they contributed, under the same terms. The
contribution terms in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) record this, and the Developer Certificate
of Origin sign-off on each commit confirms it.

## 6. What this is not

- It is not a transfer of ownership of any patent, and it grants no rights to trademarks.
- It is not a warranty that implementing the Specification does not infringe the patents
  of third parties.
- It does not cover implementations that deliberately depart from the Specification,
  beyond the parts that conform.

## 7. Relation to the licences

The Apache-2.0 licence of the schema and the examples carries its own patent licence for
those files. This commitment is broader: it covers implementing the Specification in any
way, not only copying those files.
