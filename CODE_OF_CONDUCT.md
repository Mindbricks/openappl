# Code of conduct

OpenAPPL adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, as the code of conduct for
everyone who takes part in the project: in this repository, its issues and discussions,
on [openappl.org](https://openappl.org), and in any other OpenAPPL space.

> **Before publishing:** replace this section with the official text of the Contributor
> Covenant 2.1, copied unchanged from
> https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/, and fill its
> enforcement contact with the address below.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **openappl@mindbricks.com**. Reports are read by the
steward, [Mindbricks Labs](https://mindbricks.com), and handled confidentially. The
steward applies the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
